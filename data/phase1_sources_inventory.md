# Phase 1: Sources Inventory

Metadata for each source used in the pitch-hue mapping collection.

---

## scriabin_primary — Alexander Scriabin

- **Name:** Alexander Scriabin
- **Year:** 1911 (Prometheus: Poem of Fire Op. 60; annotations 1913)
- **Type:** composer/theorist
- **Completeness:** complete
- **Coverage:** 12/12 pitch classes
- **Reference:** Peacock, K. (1985). Synesthetic Perception: Alexander Scriabin's Color Hearing. *Music Perception*, 2(4), 483-505. Luce part; Paris BN annotated score.
- **Notes:** Circle of fifths-based system; clavier à lumières. Some chromatics (D#, A#, B flat) described as gray/steel/rose; confidence medium for those.

---

## newton_optics — Isaac Newton

- **Name:** Isaac Newton
- **Year:** 1704 (Opticks)
- **Type:** scientist/theorist
- **Completeness:** complete for diatonic
- **Coverage:** 7/12 pitch classes (C, D, E, F, G, A, B — Dorian mode)
- **Reference:** Newton, I. *Opticks* (1704). Dorian mode colour wheel; Starboard Quest, Oxford Humanitas lecture.
- **Notes:** Seven spectrum colours (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet) mapped to seven notes; analogy not experimentally derived. Chromatic notes not specified.

## rimington_1912 — A. Wallace Rimington

- **Name:** A. Wallace Rimington
- **Year:** 1912
- **Type:** theorist
- **Completeness:** complete
- **Coverage:** 12/12 pitch classes
- **Reference:** Rimington, A.W. *Colour-Music: The Art of Mobile Colour* (1912). Appendix comparative table; Wikisource.
- **Notes:** Spectrum-based keyboard colour-organ; middle C = lowest red. Verbal descriptions approximate; colours cannot be fully specified in words per author.

## synesthesia_nature_2017 — Pitch class-color synesthesia (empirical)

- **Name:** Pitch class-color synesthesia (empirical)
- **Year:** 2017
- **Type:** research
- **Completeness:** complete for diatonic
- **Coverage:** 7/12 pitch classes (C–B; do–si)
- **Reference:** Itoh, K. et al. Musical pitch classes have rainbow hues in pitch class-color synesthesia. *Scientific Reports* 7, 17781 (2017). 15 synesthetes.
- **Notes:** Rainbow progression: do=red, re=yellow, mi–la intermediate, si=violet; saturation decreases. Note-name driven; pitch height maps to brightness. Chromatic (C#, D#, etc.) not fully reported; enharmonics differ by name.

## castel_1740 — Louis-Bertrand Castel

- **Name:** Louis-Bertrand Castel
- **Year:** 1740 (L'Optique des couleurs); ocular harpsichord proposed 1725
- **Type:** theorist
- **Completeness:** complete
- **Coverage:** 12/12 pitch classes
- **Reference:** Castel, L.-B. L'Optique des couleurs (Paris, 1740). Ocular harpsichord (clavecin oculaire); 12 prismatic colors to 12 tones; five primary tonics C,D,E,G,A = blue, green, yellow, red, purple.
- **Notes:** Full 12-note mapping inferred from documented five tonics plus spectrum order. Castel opposed Newton's formulation.

## ward_cognition_2006 — Ward et al. (sound-colour synesthesia)

- **Name:** Ward et al. (sound-colour synesthesia)
- **Year:** 2006
- **Type:** research
- **Completeness:** inferred (no published 12-note table)
- **Coverage:** 7/12 pitch classes (C–B; inferred from study tendencies)
- **Reference:** Ward, J. et al. Sound-Colour Synaesthesia: to What Extent Does it Use Cross-Modal Mechanisms Common to us All? Cognition (2006). Pitch-to-lightness; consistency in pitch-colour matches.
- **Notes:** Study reports pitch-height to brightness and pitch-class to hue consistency; no aggregate note-to-colour table published. Mappings here are inferred (low confidence) for inclusion; treat as tendency data only.
